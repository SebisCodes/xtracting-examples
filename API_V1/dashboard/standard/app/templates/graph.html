Connection graph

About this page

One entity in the middle and what it is connected to. Click a node to draw its own connections around it.

A bucket name starts from every member at once.

Clicking a node that is already open closes it again: everything that hangs only from it goes, and anything a second open node reaches stays. The sentence above the drawing says how many of each.

A node can be dragged where you want it and stays there. A dragged node is pinned until you drag it again or press Reset, which also fits the whole graph. With a node focused, the arrow keys move it and Enter opens or closes it.

From and To draw only the connections the archive dates inside that range. The end date is included.

What the shapes mean
  • The number in a bubble is how many connections are drawn to it. The sentence above the drawing counts the same lines.
  • A dashed ring with a “+” is not opened yet. Click it, or press Enter on it, to draw its own connections.
  • A solid ring is already opened.
  • A dashed line joins two entities that were both on the graph already.
