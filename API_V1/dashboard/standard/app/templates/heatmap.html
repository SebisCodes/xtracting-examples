Heatmap

About this page

Where the addresses in this project cluster, counted on a grid of about 110 metres and painted as a heat field. Name a place to count only there.

The place field takes a city, a country or an address from the archive itself. The entity field narrows the same count; the two are used together.

The entity field reads its term two ways. Object is one entity or one bucket. Type is an event type: the addresses counted are those of every entity the events of that kind are about.

From and To count only the locations the archive dates inside that range. The end date is included.

Heat radius is how wide one location is painted. With Only the visible area ticked, panning the map counts again.

The busiest spots are listed beside the map with their counts. A click on the map, or on one of those rows, reads out the spot and opens what is inside it: the entities standing there, the ones with the most locations first, a bucket as one row under its own name. The list loads more as you scroll it.

A spot is a cell of a grid of about 110 metres - what is drawn is a soft field, so nothing on the map has a straight edge.

Scrolling over the map zooms it; the + and − buttons do the same.
