About this page

The rows of {{ ctx.project or 'this project' }} ({{ ctx.language or '-' }}), one kind at a time, newest first.

Pick a tab; only the open one is loaded, a page at a time as you scroll. Name and Type narrow the rows to one thing or one class of thing; Search runs when you press Search, and the tab and the sort change the rows at once.

Most rows and Fewest rows group the rows by name and count them; each group opens into its rows. Every document row opens into everything the archive read out of that document.

Buckets lists the buckets whose members appear in the answer, each opening into the documents that mention its members.
