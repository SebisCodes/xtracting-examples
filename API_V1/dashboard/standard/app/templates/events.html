So: one switch with two ways to ask, each with the suggestion list that fits it (/api/suggest/entity_or_bucket, /api/suggest/event_type), a date range with presets, and cards that show the entities as links. What happened, by the entity it is about or by its type. Newest first, by the date the event itself carries.

A bucket name finds every member of the bucket.

A date preset sets the start and leaves the end open, so an event announced for next month stays visible.

{#- THE SAME SHAPE AS THE QUERY PAGE, and the same switch as every other view. It was its own thing in both respects: a label reading "Look up events" over a switch of "by entity" / "by type", stacked ABOVE the field, inside a bare div rather than a named group. Two things came of that. The wording was this page's alone - every other view calls the same control "Search by" and names its halves after what they search. And the switch sitting above the field pushed the field a label-row lower than "From" and "To" beside it, so the three controls a reader fills in were on two different lines for no reason anybody could see. Now: two groups side by side, as on the Query page, each with a legend, each a toolbar whose label row is the same height - so the entity field and the two dates sit on one line. -#}

Buckets come first, their hint begins with the word "bucket", and the entities a bucket holds are IN that row rather than offered beside it (routers/api_suggest.py: BUCKETED_KINDS). limit=8, not the macro's 12: with the "- every entity -" reset on top, twelve suggestions make thirteen rows, and NN/g puts the useful maximum at ten - past that a list stops being scanned and starts being read. -#}
